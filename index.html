Reading these numbers
  • Construction values are declared by the applicant and are not appraised. About 84% of permits carry a usable figure, so totals understate real activity.
  • Counts cover main permits only. Plumbing, drain and HVAC permits that duplicate a main permit on the same project are excluded, since one project can otherwise be counted four times. Standalone trade jobs are kept.
  • 9,082 permits have no postal code and appear under Unknown. Every number on this page comes from the gold layer table permits.gold.ui_summary.
  • Snapshot taken 14 September 2026 from City of Toronto Open Data. 152,937 permits applied for since 2020.
